Pricing FAQ

What exactly does VIP add?

Time: batch ZIPs (a category or all 244 effects at once), Godot 4 .tres and Phaser atlas packs, effect+sound sets, APNG / animated WebP / frame sequences, and 4×–8× scales. Free single-effect exports stay free — VIP never takes them away.

How do I get my license key?

Checkout opens on Waffo's hosted page (card details never touch PixJuice). After payment you return here with your key and VIP turns on immediately; the same key re-activates VIP on any browser.

Can I use the effects in a commercial game?

Yes — with or without VIP. The effects are pixelfx by Patch & Hugo Duprez (MIT); keep the license notice that ships inside every export. VIP buys convenience, not extra rights.

What happens when yearly / monthly ends?

The VIP exports lock again; everything you already downloaded keeps working — the files are yours. Lifetime never expires.
