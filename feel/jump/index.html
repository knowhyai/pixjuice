Practice room

Focus the room. Move with ←/→ or A/D and jump with Space or ↑. On touch, hold the on-screen buttons. Tap Jump for a short hop or hold it for a higher jump.

Repeatable trials

Run a trial with Timers off, then replay with Page default. The edge trial presses Jump 75 ms after walking off. The landing trial presses Jump shortly before touching down. Both use the same room and movement rules as manual play.

The selected mode drives the room and downloads. The fields edit My tune. Reset room restarts play and keeps your settings.

Coyote time

An unused jump stays available briefly after walking off a ledge. The platform collision stays the same.

Jump buffer

Press just before landing. If the press is still fresh when you land, it becomes one jump.

Jump height and gravity

Releasing Jump caps upward speed when variable height is on. Gravity applies independently. These values are starting points for this room.

Export and hookup

JSON and GDScript use the active comparison values. A share link always keeps your editable tune.

Hookup & limits

Attach the reference to a CharacterBody2D with a collision shape and a jump input action. Supply horizontal velocity in your own controller. The sample uses floor contact from move_and_slide() and is_on_floor(). Correct floor detection is a prerequisite. For similar timing, use a 120 Hz physics tick. The web demo advances in fixed steps and processes at most 100 ms per displayed frame.
