Movement feel lab

Practice room

Focus the room. Move with ←/→ or A/D, and hop with Space or ↑. On touch, hold the arrows and tap Hop. Release the arrows to leave a stop trace.

Compare movement

The selected mode drives the room and downloads. The fields edit My tune. Reset room restarts play and keeps your settings.

Acceleration and speed

Hold a direction to build speed. Acceleration also governs how quickly you reverse direction. These settings are starting points for this room.

Ground braking

Build speed, then release before the wall. The trace measures travel since release. A wall stop is marked separately. At zero brake the body coasts until it hits a wall.

Air control (%)

From rest, hop first, then hold an arrow. At 0% you keep your takeoff speed in the air. At 100% steering uses full ground acceleration. Braking on release starts when you land.

Movement settings

JSON uses the selected mode. Share links always keep My tune. The room uses 120 Hz fixed steps and processes at most 100 ms per frame.
