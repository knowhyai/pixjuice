Pixel art upscaler FAQ

How do I upscale pixel art without blurring it?

Never use a smooth resize filter: upscale by an integer factor with nearest-neighbor to keep every pixel a sharp square, or use an edge-aware algorithm like Scale2x or xBR that only touches diagonal edges. This tool does all of them in your browser.

What is the difference between Scale2x and xBR?

Scale2x only fills the stair-step corners of each 2× block, so flat areas stay perfectly crisp. xBR-style blending goes further and interpolates colors along detected edges, giving smoother curves at the cost of a few in-between colors.

What scale should I pick for my sprite?

Match a whole integer: 2× or 3× for engines and editors (then let the engine scale the rest), 4×–8× for screenshots, store pages and social posts. Non-integer scaling is what causes uneven pixel sizes, so this tool only offers integer multiples.

Can I use the upscaler on photos too?

You can, but the algorithms are made for hard-edged pixel art: nearest just makes big squares, and Scale2x/xBR have little effect on soft gradients. Convert the photo to pixel art first, then upscale it here.

Is the pixel art upscaler free?

Yes — all algorithms (nearest, Scale2x, Scale3x, xBR-style) and PNG export up to 8× are free, with no account. Everything runs locally in your browser.
